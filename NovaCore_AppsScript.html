Why it's written this way

Baked into every generated post — not something you have to remember:

Hook first. LinkedIn shows ~2 lines before "see more" — the whole first sentence has to earn the click.
Dwell time over links. Native text/carousel posts get pushed harder than posts with an outbound link in the body — put links in the first comment instead.
3–5 hashtags, not 15. Over-tagging reads as spam to the ranking model and to people.
Ask one real question. Comments in the first hour matter more than likes — end with something people can actually answer.
